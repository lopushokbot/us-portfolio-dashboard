Methodology & sources

Companies: the ten largest members of each GICS sector of the S&P 500 by market capitalisation (share classes counted once), re-ranked on every refresh — 110 companies.

Trailing P/E = latest price ÷ diluted GAAP EPS summed over the four most recently reported quarters, taken as filed from SEC EDGAR (10-Q/10-K, or the earnings release on report day) with Yahoo Finance filling gaps. GAAP includes one-off gains and charges, so a trailing multiple can look unusually high or low after a large write-down or asset sale — hover a trailing figure to see the four quarters behind it. Forward P/E = latest price ÷ next-twelve-months consensus EPS, a time-weighted blend of the current and next fiscal year's consensus. Negative earnings show as a loss and are left out of medians.

Latest price = the last trade in any session — regular, after-hours (4–8 pm ET), overnight (8 pm–4 am ET) or pre-market — with the move versus the regular close underneath. Sector P/E (all members) is computed bottom-up across every S&P 500 member of the sector at the official close; top-10 P/E = Σ market cap ÷ Σ earnings over the ten leaders; vs sector compares a company's forward P/E with its sector's all-member forward P/E (green = cheaper).

Refreshed automatically every morning (Dubai time) together with the portfolio page. Numbers are for reference only — not investment advice.
