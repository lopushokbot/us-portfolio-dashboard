Peer groups

How each benchmark is built: the median of members' own multiples at today's prices, with the simple mean for reference.

Notes

  1. Peer benchmark = median. Tesla trades near 320× trailing / 160× forward, which would push a simple Magnificent 7 mean to ~67× / ~43× — not a usable yardstick. Peer benchmarks therefore use the median of the members' multiples; the plain mean is shown in the Peer groups panel for reference. For two-member groups (HOOD·IBKR, V·MA) median and mean are identical.
  2. NU — price-to-book. As a bank, Nubank is valued on P/B: price ÷ book value per share of $2.74 (Q2 2026: $13.25B equity, 4.83B shares), compared with the P/B of JPM, BAC, WFC and GS (book value per share from each bank's Q2 2026 release). On earnings, NU trades at FY2027 consensus EPS of $1.14.
  3. GOOG & AMZN — inflated trailing EPS. Q2 2026 included one-time investment gains (Alphabet ~$99B, Amazon ~$53B pre-tax, largely Anthropic stakes), so trailing P/E is flattered — for the stocks and for the Magnificent 7 median. Forward P/E on FY2027 consensus is the cleaner read.
  4. META — depressed trailing EPS. Q3 2025 carried a one-time tax charge (EPS $1.05), so trailing P/E overstates the multiple slightly.
  5. COIN. Loss-making over the trailing twelve months (−$3.73 TTM EPS) — trailing P/E not meaningful. Forward uses FY2027 consensus of $2.88. Peers: Robinhood and Interactive Brokers.
  6. CRCL. Peers: Visa and Mastercard (payment networks). Consensus sees FY2027 EPS below FY2026, hence forward P/E above trailing.
  7. TEM. Unprofitable trailing (−$1.44) and roughly breakeven FY2027 consensus (−$0.02) — both multiples not meaningful. First positive quarter posted in Q2 2026.
  8. IREN. No clean listed peer group; benchmarked to the S&P 500 Financials sector (GICS classifies crypto miners there). Trailing EPS $0.54 predates the FY2026 results due August 27, 2026; FY2027 consensus is a loss (−$1.00), so forward is not meaningful.
  9. Forward basis. Forward EPS is next-fiscal-year consensus (FY/CY2027 — for MSFT, MU, SNDK, AAPL and V those fiscal years span roughly the next twelve months). NVDA uses next-twelve-months consensus of $11.71, since its FY2027 ends January 2027.
  10. EPS growth & PEG. Growth is the analyst consensus long-term EPS growth rate (3–5 years, % per year). PEG = forward P/E ÷ that growth rate — below 1 is conventionally "growth at a reasonable price". Where no long-term consensus exists (marked †), growth is next fiscal year vs the current one. PEG is N/M when growth is negative or forward earnings are not meaningful. Memory names (MU, SNDK) show very low PEGs because consensus assumes the current up-cycle persists — treat with the same caution as their forward P/E.
  11. Own-history band. Quarterly trailing P/E over the last five years (since IPO for CRCL and TEM), from reported TTM EPS and quarter-end prices; quarters with losses, or with P/E above 100× because earnings were near zero, are excluded, so the range only spans normally profitable periods. NU uses price-to-book history. Refreshed annually.

Methodology & sources

Prices refresh automatically every day at 18:30 Dubai time from Yahoo Finance (US market is open at that hour, so the quote is intraday). All multiples are recomputed from the new prices on every load. Earnings data (EPS, book value, consensus, index and sector multiples) is refreshed after each earnings season — see the date in the header.

Trailing P/E = price ÷ trailing-twelve-month diluted EPS, summed from the four most recently reported quarters. Forward P/E = price ÷ analyst consensus EPS (see note 9). PEG = forward P/E ÷ consensus long-term EPS growth (see note 10). History bands = own 5-year quarterly trailing P/E range (note 11). Peer benchmarks are the median of the members' own trailing and forward P/E (or P/B for banks), computed the same way at the same prices (see note 1).

Sector means (MU, SNDK, TSM, VST, OXY, TEM, IREN): S&P 500 GICS sector trailing P/E from WorldPERatio (Aug 26, 2026); forward P/E from sector-ETF data, Alaric Securities (Jul 29, 2026). S&P 500: trailing (GAAP) from multpl.com (Aug 26); forward from FactSet Earnings Insight (Aug 7). Nasdaq-100: WorldPERatio / NTM consensus (Aug 26). Per-stock EPS and book values from company earnings releases and stockanalysis.com (fetched Aug 27, 2026).

Numbers are point-in-time and for reference only — not investment advice.
