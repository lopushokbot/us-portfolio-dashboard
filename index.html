Notes

    Methodology & sources

    Prices come from Yahoo Finance once a day at 8:00 Dubai — the previous US session's close. Earnings are re-read in the same run, so trailing EPS, consensus and report dates update automatically after every report; the index and sector multiples are rebuilt from scratch on each run.

    Trailing P/E = price ÷ diluted GAAP EPS summed over the four most recently reported quarters (Yahoo Finance fundamentals; quote-level TTM EPS where quarters are incomplete). Forward P/E = price ÷ next-twelve-months consensus EPS — a time-weighted blend of the current and next fiscal year's consensus, the same basis FactSet uses for the market's forward P/E. P/B (banks) = price ÷ book value per share from the latest quarter. PEG = forward P/E ÷ consensus long-term EPS growth. History bands = own 5-year quarterly trailing P/E range.

    S&P 500, Nasdaq-100 and sector multiples are computed bottom-up from every constituent with the same definitions: Σ market cap ÷ Σ earnings, where each company's earnings = market cap × EPS ÷ price. Loss-makers are included, as S&P and FactSet do (the tile also shows the figure without them); dual share classes are counted once. Constituents: Wikipedia's S&P 500 list (GICS sectors) and Nasdaq's Nasdaq-100 list. Cross-check on Oct 20, 2026: S&P 500 forward 19.1× vs FactSet 19.0× (Oct 16); trailing 26.0× vs FactSet 26.0× and multpl 26.2×.

    Report dates: last report from Nasdaq, next report from Yahoo Finance (estimated until the company confirms). Growth: Zacks 3–5-year EPS growth, Simply Wall St where Zacks has none (manual, refreshed with this table's source date). Numbers are for reference only — not investment advice.
